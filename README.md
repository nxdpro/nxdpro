**Your website can be "up" while customers can't buy, sign up or contact you.** [JourneyGuard](https://github.com/nxdpro/journeyguard) walks through checkout and forms like a real customer and tells you, in plain English, Greek or Spanish, what broke and the likely cause. Free, open source (MIT) and runs on your own server.

```bash
# Check your own shop once: nothing is bought, no message is sent
docker run --rm ghcr.io/nxdpro/journeyguard check https://your-shop.com
```

- **We maintain what we build.** A launch is the start, not the end.

- **Security and privacy by default.** HTTPS, backups, least-privilege access and GDPR-aware setups from day one.
